Common Questions

What people actually ask us.

What is the best free astrology app in India?

Astrolya is a free astrology app that connects you with live Vedic astrologers for chat and call consultations, plus free Kundli generation, daily horoscope, palm and face reading, tarot, and remedies - all in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam.

How can I generate my free Kundli online?

Open Astrolya and enter your exact birth date, time and place - the app calculates your real Vedic birth chart (Kundli) instantly, free of cost.

Can I talk to a real astrologer online for free?

Yes - Astrolya connects you with live, real astrologers (not automated bots) across Vedic, KP, Nadi, Numerology, Tarot, Psychic and Vastu specialties for chat and call consultations.

Is palm and face reading in Astrolya accurate and private?

Astrolya's palm and face reading sends your photo securely to our AI reading engine for analysis and is not stored or shared beyond generating your reading, which is personalized to what your palm/face actually shows.

Does Astrolya support Kundli matching for marriage?

Yes - Astrolya's astrologers provide Kundli matching (Kundli Milan) and compatibility reports to check marriage compatibility between two birth charts.

What languages does Astrolya support?

Astrolya is available in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, so you can read your horoscope and chat with astrologers in the language you're most comfortable with.
